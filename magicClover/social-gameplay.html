3. SELF-ASSESSMENT

3.1. If you are concerned that your own gameplay, or the gameplay of someone you know, may be becoming difficult to control, consider the following questions:

  1. Do you regularly think about previous gameplay experiences or frequently plan future gameplay sessions?
  2. Do you hide or misrepresent the amount of time or money you spend playing?
  3. Do you become unusually angry or irritated when your gameplay is interrupted?
  4. Have you ever taken a break from gameplay and then returned to excessive or uncontrolled play?
  5. Do you use gameplay to cope with stress, sadness, anger, or other emotional difficulties?
  6. Do you frequently play casino-style or similar games late at night or early in the morning?
  7. Do you look for opportunities to play when you are away from home?
  8. Do you set gameplay limits and then repeatedly exceed them?
  9. Do you lose sleep because of gameplay?
  10. Have you been late for work, missed work, or skipped school because of gameplay?
  11. Have you stopped playing or removed a game, only to return to it later?
  12. Do you experience guilt or regret regarding your gameplay?
  13. Has gameplay contributed to arguments or problems in your relationships?
  14. Has gameplay replaced hobbies, sports, exercise, or other activities that you previously enjoyed?
  15. Have you missed appointments, responsibilities, or deadlines because of gameplay?
  16. Do you become defensive when other people express concern about the amount of time you spend playing?
  17. Has the amount of time you spend playing increased over time?
  18. Do you avoid or cancel social activities in order to play?
  19. Has gameplay negatively affected your relationships with friends or family?
  20. Do you experience unusually intense emotional reactions, including extreme excitement, sadness, anger, or fear, while playing?

3.2. The more questions you answer "yes" to, the greater the possibility that your gameplay habits may require additional attention.
